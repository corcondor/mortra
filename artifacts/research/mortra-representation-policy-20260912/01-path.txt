WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED, AND ONE EARLIER CLAIM CORRECTED
==============================================================================

  CORRECTION. An earlier report in this line of work credited the whole
  reduction from 10227 derivative entries to 0 to the learned differential
  representation. With the ladder measured, that attribution was wrong:
      memoisation alone removed 10187 of those entries
      the repository own holonomic route removed the remaining 40, and takes 0 derivative entries and 0 primitive calls of its own
      the learned representation removed 0 beyond that, and cost 39 more primitive calls than the existing route
  The mathematical result stands; the attribution of the saving does not.

  - No claim that these representations are the smallest possible.
  - No claim that depth reduction is a goal. A ratio at or near 1 is kept.
  - No claim about any architecture. `sequential depth` is only the number
    of times the same compute core must run in sequence.
  - An admission that rests on a depth-bounded search is not a proof, and
    the certificate says which kind each check was.
  - The answers at the longer lengths were not enumerated. They rest on the
    certificate, whose own scope is recorded with them.
  - Kinematic routes were not instrumented for internal arithmetic, so their
    flop proxy is null rather than estimated.
  - Nothing here was committed.
