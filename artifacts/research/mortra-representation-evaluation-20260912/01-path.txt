WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED
==========================================================================

  - No claim that these representations are the smallest possible. The fold
    closure is minimal among LINEAR spaces containing the observation; the
    differential recurrence is the one the annihilating operator gives.
  - No claim that depth reduction is the goal. Two of the five comparisons
    have a depth ratio at or near 1 and are kept as they are.
  - No claim about any architecture. `sequential depth` is used only as the
    number of times the same compute core must run in sequence.
  - The kinematic baselines' internal arithmetic was not instrumented, so
    their flop proxy is null rather than estimated.
  - A zero derivative count is a statement about entries to sympy.Expr.diff
    during that run. The tests include a positive control so that a zero is
    distinguishable from a broken instrument.
