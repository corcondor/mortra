# Parallel-transversal perpendicular-circle chart

## Theorem

Two parallel lines cut the sides of triangle ABC at Xi,Yi,Zi. For each i, intersect the perpendiculars to AC through Yi and to AB through Zi, the perpendiculars to BC through Xi and to AB through Zi, and the perpendiculars to BC through Xi and to AC through Yi.  The circumcircles of the two resulting triangles are tangent.  Every common point lies on their line of centres.

## Representation changes

- A parallel family becomes x+h*y=tau.
- All six perpendicular intersections are affine-linear in tau.
- Each circumcircle has coefficients affine-linear in tau.
- Two-circle tangency is one radius/centre discriminant.

## Exact tangency identity

`(D^2-R1^2-R2^2)^2-4*R1^2*R2^2 = 0`
